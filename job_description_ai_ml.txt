AI/ML Engineer Intern

We are building applied machine learning systems that go from a raw dataset to a
model running in production. The intern will sit with the engineers who own the
model, not on a separate research island. Expect real, messy data, a fast
feedback loop, and code review from people who have shipped models before.

Day to day this looks like reading a recent paper on Monday, having a rough
prototype by Thursday, and arguing about whether the numbers are real on Friday.

Who we are looking for:

- The candidate should have a minimum CGPA of 8.00 out of 10, or an equivalent
  of 80% where the institute grades in percentage
- The candidate should be proficient in Python
- The candidate must have hands-on experience with at least one deep learning
  framework, such as PyTorch, TensorFlow or Keras
- The candidate should have built classical machine learning models, such as a
  regression model or a classification model
- The candidate should have built something hands-on in computer vision or in
  natural language processing
- The candidate should quote concrete evaluation numbers for their own work,
  such as an accuracy, an F1 score, an R2 value or an error rate
- The candidate should have a working knowledge of data structures and algorithms
- The candidate should have held a position of responsibility in a student club,
  student team, technical society, committee or similar body

Good to have:

- Experience with large language models, in any one of retrieval-augmented
  generation, LangChain, vector databases, embeddings or fine-tuning
- Exposure to MLOps and deployment, in any one of Docker, FastAPI, MLflow,
  model serving or experiment tracking
- Hands-on reinforcement learning work
- Research exposure, such as working in a lab, working under a professor, or a
  literature survey of recent papers
- Experience with a cloud platform such as AWS, GCP or Azure

Other conditions:

- The candidate must have no current backlogs
- The candidate should be willing to relocate to Bengaluru for the internship
- The candidate should be available full time for six months starting January
- Expected stipend should be within the advertised band
